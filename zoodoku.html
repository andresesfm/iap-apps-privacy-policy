Privacy Policy for Zoodoku: Animal Sudoku

Last Updated: September 4, 2026

Introduction

Zoodoku is a sudoku puzzle game in which animals take the place of numbers. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the app.

Information We Collect

Zoodoku does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers. All game data — your current level and progress, remaining tries, preferences, and whether ads have been removed — is kept locally on your device and never leaves it. The app has no accounts, no registration, and no analytics of its own.

Advertising

The free version of Zoodoku displays ads provided by Google AdMob, including banner and interstitial ads. You may also optionally watch a rewarded video ad to earn extra tries; watching is always voluntary. AdMob may collect and use device advertising identifiers and usage data for ad serving and measurement, governed by Google's own Privacy Policy, which is separate from ours.

In-App Purchases

Zoodoku offers consumable in-app products that grant extra tries (packs of 3, 10, and 25) and a non-consumable "Remove Ads" upgrade. Payments are processed by Google Play; we do not receive or store your payment details.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
