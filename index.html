Privacy Policy for QR Image Reader

Last Updated: August 29, 2026

Introduction

QR Image Reader reads QR codes from images already on your device. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the app.

Information We Collect

QR Image Reader is designed with your privacy in mind. The images you select and the QR-code contents they contain are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded to us or stored on our servers.

  • Scan history is stored locally on your device only.
  • We do not collect, transmit, or store the content of scanned codes.

Advertising

The free version of QR Image Reader displays ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device advertising identifiers and usage data for ad serving and measurement, governed by Google's own Privacy Policy, which is separate from ours.

In-App Purchases

You may purchase an "Ad-Free" upgrade as a non-consumable in-app product. Payments are processed by Google Play; we do not receive or store your payment details.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
